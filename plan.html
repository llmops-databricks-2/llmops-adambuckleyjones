Get the PDF into Databricks and parse it

Done · PR #2 in review

  1. The PDF is in the repo root, Laws of the Game 2026_27_single pages.pdf (260 pages, 22 MB). Keep it out of git: it's over the 1 MB pre-commit limit and IFAB-copyrighted.

  2. Change volume: "arxiv_files" to "laws_files" in project_config.yml for all three environments, and update tests/test_config.py to match.

  3. Create the volume and upload the PDF:

    databricks volumes create llmops_dev adambuckleyjones laws_files MANAGED -p llmops-course
databricks fs mkdir dbfs:/Volumes/llmops_dev/adambuckleyjones/laws_files/raw -p llmops-course
databricks fs cp "Laws of the Game 2026_27_single pages.pdf" dbfs:/Volumes/llmops_dev/adambuckleyjones/laws_files/raw/laws_of_the_game_2026_27.pdf -p llmops-course

  4. Parse it in notebooks/1_parse_laws.py, on serverless:

    CREATE OR REPLACE TABLE llmops_dev.adambuckleyjones.laws_parsed AS
SELECT path, ai_parse_document(content) AS parsed, current_timestamp() AS parsed_at
FROM READ_FILES('/Volumes/llmops_dev/adambuckleyjones/laws_files/raw/', format => 'binaryFile');

  5. Inspect the output: element and page counts, whether the "Law 1" to "Law 17" headings come out as separate elements, and what happened to the offside diagrams.

Done when

laws_parsed has one row, the text of Law 11 is readable in it, and you know what the headings look like. Stage 2 depends on that.

Status (14 Oct): done. 260 pages, 1,742 elements, no errors. What the parse looks like:

  • Each Law is a title element ("Offside"), preceded by a text element with the number ("Law11"). Page footers also carry it ("Law 11 | Offside").
  • Sections are numbered section_header elements ("1. Offside position", "2. Offside offence"), so Law 11.1 keys come straight from the headers.
  • Section text continues across pages after page_footer and page_number elements, so assign text to sections by element order and drop footers, page numbers and figures.
  • The 256 figures are mostly IFAB logos and photos, with no offside diagrams in Law 11.
  • The course's _extract_chunks keeps only text elements and would lose every heading, so Stage 2 needs its own splitter.
  • The PDF already contains "Outline summary of Law changes" (p166) and "Details of all Law changes" (p169–186), plus the glossary (p194–207) and the practical guidelines (p208 onwards).
