What changed

  • Fixed-size chunks found more. 5/5 with every method, the answer always ranked first. Section chunks missed the goal-line handball question: the DOGSO answer sits in a long chunk (12.4-8) that also covers other sending-off rules, so its embedding matches less closely.
  • Filtering by Law fixed the miss. Restricting the section search to Law 12 brought 12.4-8 into the top 3 (third). Filtering needs the Law to be known, for example from the question or a first search.
  • Hybrid changed almost nothing. plainto_tsquery requires every word of the question, so full-sentence questions score 0 on full text and ranking falls back to the vector score. Only the one-word query "DOGSO" got a text score (0.1). Next: use websearch_to_tsquery with OR terms, or key terms only.
  • The heading prefix helps. "DOGSO" is a hit for every method because each chunk starts with its law, section and subheading.
  • Trade-off. Fixed chunks are smaller and more precise, but cut rules mid-sentence and give the model less context per chunk; 261 chunks against 138. A middle option: section chunks with a lower word limit, so long blocks like DOGSO are split.
